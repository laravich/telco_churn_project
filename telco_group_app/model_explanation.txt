COMPARISON OF LAVANYA’S AND DANIAL’S MODELS

Both models use logistic regression to predict whether a customer will stay or churn.

1. CUSTOMER INFORMATION

Lavanya:
Keeps the original customer information and adds three indicators:
- Number of internet add-ons.
- Whether payment is automatic.
- Whether the customer has been subscribed for six months or less and has a month-to-month contract.

Danial:
Simplifies the contract and payment categories instead of adding those indicators.

2. CONTRACT TYPES

Lavanya:
Keeps month-to-month, one-year and two-year contracts separate.

Danial:
Combines one-year and two-year contracts into “Yearly.”
Keeps month-to-month contracts separate.

3. PAYMENT METHODS

Lavanya:
Keeps the original payment methods and adds an automatic-payment indicator.

Danial:
Keeps electronic checks separate and groups the other payment methods together.

4. MISSING VALUES

Lavanya:
Sets missing total charges to zero for customers with zero months of tenure.

Danial:
Estimates missing numerical values using similar customers.

5. MODEL TRAINING

Lavanya:
Gives churners extra weight during training.
Uses 80% of the data for training and 20% for testing.

Danial:
Uses default weighting.
Uses 70% of the data for training and 30% for testing.

6. PREDICTION CUTOFF

Lavanya:
Flags churn when the model’s churn score is 0.57 or higher.

Danial:
Flags churn when the model’s churn score is 0.50 or higher.

7. HOW THE APP COMPARES THEM

The app gives both models the same customer details.
Each model prepares those details using its own method.
The app displays their scores and decisions side by side.

Different features, training settings and cutoffs can lead to different predictions. The scores are estimates, not guarantees.

8. IMPORTANT LIMITATION

This comparison describes how the models work, not which is better.
A fair performance comparison requires the same test data and evaluation metrics.

Danial’s exported logistic regression is provisional until he confirms his final model.
